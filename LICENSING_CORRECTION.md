# EvoRig Licensing Correction and Owner's Statement

**Date:** November 17, 2026  
**Repository:** https://github.com/thanks-cohn/EvoRig  
**Original concept originator / project owner:** thanks-cohn (@thanks-cohn)  
**Current license declaration:** [LICENSE](LICENSE) — all rights reserved, prior written permission required to use protected materials.

## What happened

An AI agent helping establish the EvoRig repository published an initial
`LICENSE` file that described the founding documentation as Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), in commit
`2c70381fd741f88257f5f958c168239437536b76`.

## Owner's correction of intent

The owner states that:
1. The owner requested that he be properly credited for the original EvoRig idea.
2. The owner **did not ask for or approve selection of CC BY 4.0**, or authorize an open-ended public license allowing reuse of copyrightable work upon attribution.
3. The agent selected and published that license based on a misunderstanding of the owner's instructions, before the owner had completed clarifying his wishes.
4. The owner intended to reserve control over protected project materials and to require the owner's express prior written permission before any reuse requiring authorization.
5. Immediately after recognizing the mismatch, the owner directed that the mistaken CC BY 4.0 license be removed as the *current* repository's license and corrected.

The owner therefore **disputes that the AI-agent-posted notice constituted
an authorized CC BY 4.0 grant**. The owner does not ratify or consent
to that purported grant by this correction. This record is intended to
preserve the owner's contemporaneous objection and stated lack of consent.

## Actions taken

- The current `LICENSE` was replaced on November 17, 2026, in commit
  `50fa7d4454a573363c97b452a27c63b1c7bafdde`.
- The root `NOTICE` and `README.md` were changed to remove the earlier
  grant assertions and state the owner's current permission-only position.
- The prior mistaken version may remain in Git commit history, forks,
  external caches, and previously obtained copies.

## Legal limits — do not misstate

This correction **does not itself prove that the earlier license was
legally void or extinguish rights, if any, that vested from a legally
effective earlier grant**. The owner contends that the agent lacked
authorization to offer CC BY 4.0. Whether the earlier notice had legal
effect depends on the full facts and relevant law. Creative Commons
licenses are generally irrevocable if validly granted.

The current notice does not grant permission to use original protected
EvoRig materials. It also cannot prevent independent use of abstract,
unprotected ideas, functional methods, or other acts permitted by law.
Protection for a technical invention, if available, generally requires
separate patent analysis and filings; copyright cannot reserve the
underlying engineering concept as such.

For the strongest evidence, retain original chat instructions, correction
instructions, repository history, timestamps, and copies of the relevant
materials. Qualified intellectual-property counsel should evaluate the
prior publication and potential patent-filing implications.

This document reports the owner's stated position rather than a judicial
finding or an independent legal opinion.
